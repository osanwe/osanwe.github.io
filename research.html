Research & Media

I've been working on weather forecasting for 6+ years. When I started to dive into this domain, my primary task was precipitation nowcasting, and I was solving it with deep learning. Now I'm responsible for the entire forecasting system and ML models used in it. So I'm interested in different parts of ML based weather forecasting, and want to develop the weather forecasting system as well as possible.

In my experience, I've noticed some patterns when ML models fail at weather forecasting, and I think that adding physical information into them is a promising direction of research, because when a model works with the physical world it's not enough to learn correlations from data. The model needs to understand how the physical world works, and how to integrate that knowledge into ML models is an open research question.
